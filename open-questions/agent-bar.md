# The agent in the bar: the clock's pill, or the music island?

**Question:** "the top bar with the music will have a different style that says omawin is listening": while the agent (Ctrl+Win+H, docs/agent.md) listens and works, where does it show?

**Options:**
1. The clock's pill, like dictation (Win+H), in a colour of its own: "omawin is listening", then "omawin is working: <step>", then the answer.
2. The music island: the now-playing text gives way to the agent while it runs.

**Default:** 1, the clock's pill.
