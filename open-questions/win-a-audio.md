**Context:** Win+A grew from the Audio page into Quick settings on 2026-10-15. The switches already have system menu rows (Quick › Wi-Fi on / off …) and their own page.

**Options:**
1. Keep Quick settings as its own page, reached from the launcher (a "Quick settings" item, `@quick`), the system menu (Quick › All of it) and `omawin launcher quick`; no key of its own.
2. Give Quick settings a key of its own (which one is yours to say).
3. Drop the switches page; keep only the system menu rows.

**Default (done):** 1. On both pages ← → now turn the selected speaker, app or monitor down / up (Shift for 1%), and ↑ ↓ move.
